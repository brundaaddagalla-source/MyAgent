# Planet Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life.

## Key Facts:
- **Distance from Sun:** ~149.6 million km (1 AU)
- **Radius:** 6,371 km
- **Age:** ~4.54 billion years
- **Atmosphere:** ~78% Nitrogen, 21% Oxygen, 1% other gases
- **Surface:** 71% Water, 29% Land

## Features:
- Liquid water oceans
- Protective atmosphere and magnetic field
- Diverse climate zones and rich biodiversity


## Focus: India
- **Location:** South Asia
- **Capital:** New Delhi
- **Population:** ~1.4 billion (7th largest by area, most populous country)
- **Geography:** Diverse terrain including the Himalayas, Thar Desert, Indo-Gangetic Plain, and extensive coastline along the Indian Ocean.
- **Key Characteristics:** Ancient civilization, rich cultural heritage, vast biodiversity, and the world's largest democracy.
