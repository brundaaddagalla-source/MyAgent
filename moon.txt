# The Moon

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It is the fifth largest satellite in the Solar System.

## Key Facts:
- **Average Distance from Earth:** ~384,400 km
- **Radius:** 1,737.4 km
- **Orbital Period:** ~27.3 days
- **Surface Gravity:** ~1.62 m/s² (about 1/6th of Earth's gravity)

## Features:
- Cratered surface from asteroid and comet impacts
- Extreme surface temperature variations
- Tally locked with Earth (shows the same side)
