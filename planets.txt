# Detailed Overview of Solar System Planets

## Terrestrial (Rocky) Planets

### 1. Mercury
- **Distance from Sun:** ~57.9 million km
- **Radius:** 2,439.7 km
- **Orbital Period:** 88 days
- **Key Characteristics:** Smallest planet, extreme temperatures ranging from -180°C at night to 430°C during the day, heavily cratered surface with no significant atmosphere.

### 2. Venus
- **Distance from Sun:** ~108.2 million km
- **Radius:** 6,051.8 km
- **Orbital Period:** 225 days
- **Key Characteristics:** Similar size to Earth, covered in dense clouds of sulfuric acid, runaway greenhouse effect making it the hottest planet (~465°C), rotates backwards relative to most planets.

### 3. Earth
- **Distance from Sun:** ~149.6 million km
- **Radius:** 6,371 km
- **Orbital Period:** 365.25 days
- **Key Characteristics:** Only known planet with liquid water oceans and life, protective atmosphere rich in nitrogen and oxygen, active plate tectonics.

### 4. Mars
- **Distance from Sun:** ~227.9 million km
- **Radius:** 3,389.5 km
- **Orbital Period:** 687 days
- **Key Characteristics:** Known as the Red Planet due to iron oxide on its surface, home to Olympus Mons (largest volcano) and Valles Marineris (massive canyon system), thin carbon dioxide atmosphere.

## Gas & Ice Giants

### 5. Jupiter
- **Distance from Sun:** ~778.5 million km
- **Radius:** 69,911 km
- **Orbital Period:** 11.86 years
- **Key Characteristics:** Largest planet in the solar system, gas giant mainly composed of hydrogen and helium, famous for the Great Red Spot storm, has over 90 moons including Ganymede.

### 6. Saturn
- **Distance from Sun:** ~1.43 billion km
- **Radius:** 58,232 km
- **Orbital Period:** 29.45 years
- **Key Characteristics:** Famous for its stunning, complex ring system made of ice and rock particles, gas giant with low overall density (less dense than water), has major moons like Titan and Enceladus.

### 7. Uranus
- **Distance from Sun:** ~2.87 billion km
- **Radius:** 25,362 km
- **Orbital Period:** 84 years
- **Key Characteristics:** Ice giant composed of water, ammonia, and methane; rotates nearly on its side (98° tilt); cold pale blue atmosphere.

### 8. Neptune
- **Distance from Sun:** ~4.50 billion km
- **Radius:** 24,622 km
- **Orbital Period:** 164.8 years
- **Key Characteristics:** Farthest major planet, vivid blue ice giant with the fastest recorded winds in the solar system (up to 2,100 km/h), home to Triton, a moon with cryovolcanoes.
